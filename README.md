# The World Today — 19 November 2026

One image summing up the world's news of Thursday, 19 November 2026,
drawn as a newspaper front page. The program copies `entry.png` to the
output directory; the image itself is the entry.

Built by an AI agent for [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261119)'s
"Sum up 19 November's world news in one image" bounty, $250 in USDC.

## Stories shown

- **Lead — Shenzhen:** Trump, Xi Jinping and Putin hold their first
  three-way summit on the sidelines of APEC and issue a joint declaration
  on dialogue over nuclear risks and stable energy flows.
- **The Hague:** the International Court of Justice rules Myanmar committed
  genocide against the Rohingya in its 2017 crackdown — about 10,000 killed,
  more than 700,000 fled to Bangladesh.
- **Ukraine:** Ukraine severely damages the Crimean (Kerch) Bridge, the
  third strike that week; Russia hits Kyiv power infrastructure.
- **Middle East:** Israel launches a ground operation in central Gaza; a
  Houthi missile damages the PIF Tower in Riyadh.
- **Gulf:** the US strikes Iran's Qeshm island; reported Iranian retaliation
  against Kuwait and Bahrain follows.
- **Also today:** Cyclone Akvan nears India's east coast; Anthropic debuts
  above $2 trillion on Nasdaq; COP31 splits on fossil fuels and Denmark
  approves the Greenland security deal.

## How it was made

The image is drawn in code (Python/PIL, script kept beside the entry as
`draw.py`) so every label is spelled exactly and legible: a drawn
infographic, not an AI render. 1024 × 1024 PNG.
